                                                                                        4.1. Data envelope analysis

                                                                                            The Data Envelopment Analysis Method (DEA) is a method ﬁrst
                                                                                        proposed by the famous American operations researcher A.
                                                                                        Charnes and W.W. Copper in 1978 and used to evaluate the relative
                                                                                        efﬁciency. Without the need to set weights and dimensionless
                                                                                        processing of the index, the relative efﬁciency value can be ob-
                       Fig. 1. Subsidy2014 boxplot by region.                           tained by linear programming. Therefore, it is widely used in multi-
                                                                                        input and multi-output social problems. Its application areas
                                                                                        include transportation, education, energy and so on. The Charnes-
                                                                                        Cooper-Rhodes (CCR) model is one of the most representative
                                                                                        DEA models. It assumes constant returns to scale to study input-
                                                                                        output efﬁciency (Charnes et al., 1978). The CCR model can be
                                                                                        divided into input-oriented and output-oriented. The output-
                                                                                        oriented is to maximize the output without changing the amount
                                                                                        of input elements, which is suitable for the research content of this
                                                                                        paper. Therefore, the output-oriented CCR model is chosen.
