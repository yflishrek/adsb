[2] Description（300-1000 字符！注意长度要求）
————————————————————————————
This research aims to assess aviation noise exposure in the Beijing-Tianjin-Hebei (BTH) multi-airport region using FAA's Integrated Noise Model (INM) and Aviation Environmental Design Tool (AEDT). The study area covers three major airports operating in close proximity: Beijing Capital (ZBAA), Beijing Daxing (ZBAD), and Tianjin Binhai (ZBTJ). Accurate flight operations data from OpenSky is essential as primary input for noise modeling, including aircraft type, departure/arrival times, flight trajectories, and altitude profiles. The research is supervised by Prof. Zhao Yifei at the College of Air Traffic Management, Civil Aviation University of China. Expected outputs include noise exposure contour maps, a comparative analysis across the three airports, and peer-reviewed journal publications. All data will be used exclusively for non-commercial academic purposes.

[7] Required Data — Time period（最多 500 字符，时间范围描述）

January 6, 2025 to January 19, 2025 (14 consecutive days). The data will be used to analyze daily noise exposure patterns, peak/off-peak variations, and cumulative noise impact from simultaneous operations across the three airports.
